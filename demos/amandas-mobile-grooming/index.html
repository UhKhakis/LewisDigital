About

One owner, one van, since 2011

Amanda’s Mobile Grooming is a one-owner mobile grooming business — Amanda has been grooming dogs and cats around Bryan and the Brazos Valley since February 2011. There’s no shop, no drop-off desk, no other dogs barking in a waiting room — she comes to you and works around your schedule directly.

Long-time customers describe her fitting them in on short notice, working around unpredictable schedules, and handling nervous or difficult dogs with a steady hand. She's personally invested in the business she's built — when a review questioned how she runs things, she replied at length, in her own words, standing behind nine-plus years (now fifteen-plus) of established work.
