About the shop

Thirty-plus years on the same stretch of road.

Dean Plumbing & Repair has been fixing pipes around College Station for more than thirty years, per the tenure MapQuest’s own directory listing cites for the business. That’s a long run for a shop that, by every source we could find, has never had much of a website or a marketing push — just an address, a phone number, and word of mouth doing the rest.

The shop’s Facebook page describes the work in one plain line: “Residential & Commercial Plumbing and Repairs.” Not a specialty on one side of that line — houses and businesses both, which tracks for an outfit that’s outlasted three decades of trends in either direction.

We couldn’t find a staff photo, a review write-up, or an old website to pull a voice from — this page is built honestly from what’s actually public: the address, the phone number, the trade, and that one repeated fact about tenure. Full sourcing is in the README.
