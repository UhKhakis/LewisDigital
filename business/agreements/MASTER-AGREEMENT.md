## 1. What this agreement covers

This agreement, together with the Client Scope & Fee Schedule ("**the Schedule**"),
sets out the terms under which Lewis Digital builds, hosts, and cares for the
Client's online presence — a website, an AI receptionist, or both — and
administers the Client's domain name.

The Schedule names the services, the fees, and the go-live date. If anything in
the Schedule conflicts with this agreement, this agreement controls unless
both parties have written a different exception in the Schedule and the Owner
of Lewis Digital has initialed it.

This is the whole agreement between us about these services. Any change must be
in writing and signed by both parties.

## 2. The services

We will deliver the package chosen in the Schedule:

- **Basic Website** — a custom website built in your business's own brand, plus
  hosting & care.
- **AI Receptionist** — an AI service that helps answer and handle common
  customer calls or inquiries for your business, within the scope written in
  the Schedule.
- **Bundle** — Basic Website **and** AI Receptionist together, including hosting
  & care.

**Hosting & care** (part of Basic Website and Bundle) means the day-to-day
technical upkeep of your site: hosting on our stack, keeping software current,
backups, and domain-renewal administration. It is ordinary maintenance, not new
work.

**Google Business Profile.** If you need one and it's part of your package, we
will set up or optimize a Google Business Profile and feature your business
info (hours, contact, ratings) on your site. You'll keep control of that
profile.

## 3. What's billed, and when

The exact amounts are in the Schedule. Here is how billing works in general:

- **Setup fee.** The one-time setup fee (Basic Website **$299**, or Bundle
  **$399**) is collected **before** we begin production work.
- **Monthly rates.** The **AI Receptionist** (standalone) is **$199/month**.
  Website **hosting & care** is **$25/month** (Basic), or it's included in the
  **Bundle** — which is **$149/month** for hosting & care **plus** the AI
  Receptionist together.
- **Domain charge.** Your domain registration cost is collected **before** we
  buy the domain. This is a pass-through — you pay exactly what the registrar
  charges us, with **no markup**.
- **Monthly service.** Monthly service (hosting & care, AI Receptionist, or the
  Bundle monthly rate) starts on the **Go-Live Date** in the Schedule — the day
  your site or AI service is live.
- **Web hosting & care** (Basic $25/month, or included in the Bundle's $149) is
  billed monthly and runs for a **12-month initial term** from the Go-Live Date
  (see Section 4).

We'll send an invoice for each amount as it comes due and collect payment by
the method we've agreed (we use Stripe for one-time setup fees and monthly
invoicing).

**Start of work.** We don't begin production work or buy the domain until this
agreement is signed and the setup fee and domain charge are paid. Production
starts only after you've confirmed the scope in writing and the applicable
payment is in.

## 4. The 12-month hosting term

Web hosting & care (in Basic Website and Bundle) has a **12-month initial
term** that starts on the Go-Live Date. You agree to pay the monthly hosting &
care rate for the full 12 months. That's how we're able to keep your site up,
backed up, and maintained at this rate.

**After the 12 months**, the hosting & care continues month-to-month unless
either of us ends it.

## 6. Requested changes — separate from upkeep

We're glad to make changes to your site or service. Hosting & care covers
ordinary upkeep only. Anything else — changes to content, design, layout,
pages, features, or how things work — is available **anytime** and is billed
separately at **$75 per hour with a one-hour minimum** per request.

We won't start a requested change until you've **approved it in writing** and
agreed to the estimate. That way there are no surprises. We'll confirm scope and
an estimate first, then bill for actual time at the rate above.

## 7. Your domain — owned by you

Your domain name is **yours**. We buy it on your behalf at the actual GoDaddy
(or other registrar) cost, with no markup, and we manage it as part of hosting
& care.

- **Registration** is a separate pass-through charge collected before purchase.
- **Renewal** happens on your domain's real registration cycle — every one or
  two years. When renewal is coming due, we'll tell you in advance and add the
  actual renewal cost to your normal monthly invoice.
- When hosting & care ends, we'll help transfer your domain to you or to a
  registrar you choose, so you always keep it. Section 8 has the details.

## 8. Ownership and transfer

- **Your content and your site.** Everything you provide (your text, photos,
  your business info) stays yours. Once we're paid in full for the setup and
  your account is settled, you own the finished website we built for you and
  all your content on it.
- **Your domain.** The domain is registered in your name (or transferred to
  you) and stays yours. On request, and at your cost for any registrar or
  transfer fees, we'll transfer the domain to you or your chosen registrar.
- **Our tools.** Our templates, software, the AI service, and our internal
  methods remain ours. That's the machinery we use to serve you; it's not
  transferred.

## 9. Your responsibilities

You agree to:

- Give us accurate, current information (business name, hours, contact details,
  the email that should receive form submissions, and any content you want on
  the site).
- Keep your contact and billing information current.
- Make sure the content and information you give us is accurate and that you
  have the right to use it (for example, your own photos and reviews).
- Get in touch promptly when we need a decision or information to keep things
  on schedule.

## 10. The contact form

If your package includes a website, the site's contact form is set up to
deliver messages to **your business's own email address** — the one in the
Schedule. That email is yours to monitor; we'll confirm a test message actually
lands there before we call the site live. If you later change the email, just
tell us and we'll update it as part of hosting & care.

## 11. AI receptionist — what it is and isn't

The AI Receptionist is a helpful tool, not a guarantee.

- It can be wrong, and it can't cover every situation. We don't promise it will
  handle every call or every question correctly, and you shouldn't rely on it
  for medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical answers.
- It follows the scope in the Schedule. If you want it to handle more, that's a
  requested change under Section 6.
- We're not responsible for how you use it, or for any decision you make (or
  don't make) based on it.
- We make **no guarantees** of any particular result, and the site and services
  are provided "as is" and "as available" — subject to the limits in Section 13.

## 12. Confidentiality

We may see or hear business information that isn't public (customer lists,
financial numbers, business practices). We agree to keep that information
confidential and use it only to serve you. We won't share it with anyone except
as needed to deliver the services or as the law requires. Your domain and the
public site are public by nature and aren't "confidential" under this section.
