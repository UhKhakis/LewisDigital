# Lewis Digital — Client Agreement Package

Reusable, client-facing commercial agreement templates for Lewis Digital
(a DBA of **Lewis Tech Services LLC**). These are **working drafts, not legal
advice and not final contracts.**

## Files in this folder

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `MASTER-AGREEMENT.md` | The master Website, Hosting & AI Services Agreement — the full terms that govern every engagement. |
| `CLIENT-SCOPE-FEE-SCHEDULE.md` | A one-page per-client Scope & Fee Schedule you fill in for each deal (package, fees, domain, go-live date, delivery email). |
| `README.md` | This file — how to complete the package for a real client. |

## The rule you must not skip

Before either document is shown to a real client, it **must** be
1. **approved by the owner** (Jake Lewis), and
2. **reviewed by a licensed Texas attorney.**

The agreement says this on its face. Do not present any version with
placeholders, "proposed" language, or an incomplete bracket `[  ]` as final.
These templates are the team's working hypothesis, not a ratified or legally
sufficient contract.

## Pricing and terms baked in (keep consistent)

- **Basic Website**: $299 one-time + $25/month hosting & care
- **AI Receptionist**: $199/month
- **Bundle** (Website + AI Receptionist + hosting & care): $399 one-time +
  $149/month
- **Hosting & care** carries a **12-month initial term from Go-Live Date**
  (Basic and Bundle).
- **Setup fee + domain charge are collected before** production build / domain
  purchase. Monthly service begins at **go-live**.
- **Client-requested changes** (content, design, layout, pages, features,
  functionality) are **$75/hour, one-hour minimum**, only after the client
  **approves in writing**; separate from ordinary upkeep.
- **Domains are client-owned**, bought by Lewis Digital at actual GoDaddy/
  registrar cost (**no markup**). Initial registration is a separate
  pass-through; renewal runs on the real 1- or 2-year cycle, is announced in
  advance, and is added to the normal monthly invoice when due.
- **Contact forms deliver to the business's own email.**
- **No new production work** until agreement/payment as applicable.

## How to complete the package for one client

1. **Fill the Schedule first.** Open `CLIENT-SCOPE-FEE-SCHEDULE.md` and fill
   every field: business name, contact, the email that should receive form
   submissions and support, phone, chosen package, Go-Live Date, the domain
   being set up, whether a Google Business Profile is needed, the website
   pages/features, and the AI Receptionist scope. Fill in the actual domain
   cost (look it up at the registrar — it's a pass-through, no markup) and any
   exceptions. The Schedule table of fees must match the Master Agreement.
2. **Resolve the owner-decision items in the Master Agreement** (use the
   checklist at the end):
   - **Early cancellation** during the 12-month term — owner decides (e.g.
     remaining months due, a single fee, or none). Sections 5 and the checklist.
   - **Late / non-payment terms** — owner decides late fee/interest (within
     Texas limits), grace period, and suspension rules. Section 14.
   - **Month-to-month cancellation notice days** after the term. Section 5.
   - **Client-facing support email**. Section 15 — confirm the real address.
   - **Attorney completion/confirmation** of the liability (13), payment/
     suspension (14), and law/venue (17) sections — these have * placeholders.
3. **Have the owner approve** the final, fully-completed version.
4. **Present as one package.** The signed Schedule + Master Agreement together
   form the contract. Collect setup + domain before production; start monthly
   billing at go-live; email the form to the business's own inbox and confirm a
   test message before go-live.
5. **Keep a copy.** Retain the signed/e-signed package for the client file.

## Notes on voice and honesty

- The agreement is meant to be plain, warm, and small-business real — no jargon
  or pressure. Read it that way. If any sentence reads like a corporate robot,
  rewrite it.
- Domains are a client-owned pass-through at actual cost — never represented as
  a Lewis Digital fee or profit center.
- Never invent penalty, pressure, or "final notice" terms. Anything the owner
  hasn't decided and approved stays a clearly-labelled decision item or
  placeholder, never a silent binding term.
