# Lewis Digital — Phone Pitch Kit

**For owner-led phone outreach to the Bryan/College Station no-website leads.**
Written in Jake Lewis's warm, plain, small-business voice. Read this alongside `OUTREACH.md` and `outreach/PLAYBOOK.md` — this kit follows the same voice rules, demo gate, and honesty standards.

**Preparation only.** Nothing here is authorization to call. If/when the lead authorizes phone outreach, this kit is ready to run as-is.

---

## Voice rules (owner-set — must follow on every call)

- **First person as Jake Lewis.** Warm, plain, small-business real. Short sentences, contractions.
- **Never pressure, never manufacture urgency or scarcity.** No "today only", no "spots left", no "final notice".
- **No fake testimonials, no false claims, no invented facts.** Only say what is verified in `leads.csv` or the lead's public listing.
- **Never state a rating, review count, service, hour, or owner name as fact** unless it is verified in `leads.csv` or a cited public source. This kit stays strictly to what `leads.csv` records: town/address, niche, and how customers currently find the business (Google Maps listing / Facebook / GlossGenius / Instagram / directory).
- **Do NOT offer the AI Receptionist during a website pitch.** It is a separate add-on sold after the website relationship.
- **Always leave an easy out.** "No problem, I'll leave you be."
- **Phone for questions: (940) 206-1858.**

## 3. Objection handling

All replies are honest, in-voice, and never pressure or manufacture scarcity. **No AI Receptionist is offered here** (it is a separate add-on sold after the website relationship).

### "We're too busy / no time."
> "I get it, you're running a business. I can keep it really simple — I'll build the preview from the public details you already have, and you can look at it whenever you have a minute. There's no call or decision required just to see it. If now's not a good time, I'm happy to leave it there."

### "We don't need a website."
> "Totally fair. Lots of businesses get by just fine without one — right now people find you on your Google listing, and that works. A site would just give you a simple place you control with your own info. But if it's not something you want, I won't push it. Would you like to see a free preview anyway, just to have a look?"

### "Is this free?"
> "The preview is completely free and there's no obligation — nothing goes live unless you decide you want it. If you like it and choose to go ahead, the basic website is $299 to build, then $25 a month for hosting and care. But there's no charge at all just to look."

### "We already tried [a website / this before]."
> "That's understandable, and I'm not trying to tell you what to do. If it didn't work out before, a free preview costs you nothing to look at and doesn't commit you to anything. If it's not for you, that's fine — no pressure either way."

### "Not interested."
> "No problem at all, thanks for being straight with me. I'll close this out and won't follow up again. If you ever change your mind, you can reach me at (940) 206-1858. Take care." *(Mark `CLOSED — OPTED OUT` in the log. Do not re-contact.)*

### "Call me later."
> "Of course. Would you rather I leave it, or is there a time that's better for you to talk? I want to check back at a moment that actually works for you, not just whenever."

### "We do fine with walk-ins."
> "That's great — sounds like you're doing well. No need for me to change anything you don't want changed. If you'd ever like a simple place online so folks can find your info, a free preview is here whenever you want it. Otherwise, I'll leave you be."

## 5. Call-logging format

Log every call to `/home/team/shared/leads/outreach/`. **Honesty rules:** record only what actually happened on the call. Never fabricate a result, a confirmation, or a next step. If unanswered, log the truth.

Create one file per call, named `YYYY-MM-DD-<business-slug>-phone-call.md`, using this template:

```markdown
# Phone Call Log — <Business Name>

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Time:** HH:MM (CST) [leave blank if not recorded]
- **Phone:** <number from leads.csv>
- **Called by:** Jake Lewis (owner) / delegated caller
- **Answered?** Yes / No / Voicemail / Wrong number

## Outcome (pick one)
- [ ] interested-in-demo
- [ ] no-thanks
- [ ] callback
- [ ] no-answer
- [ ] left-voicemail
- [ ] wrong-number

## Notes
- What the owner said (quote if useful)
- Objection raised (if any): <e.g., "no time", "we do fine with walk-ins">
- Exact next step agreed: <e.g., "owner will call back Thursday", "I'll send demo", "none — opted out">
- Demo status after this call (per PLAYBOOK): `not offered` / `offered` / `confirmed` / `building` / `ready` / `approved`
- Any follow-up date agreed, or clear stop status
- Any facts the owner verified or corrected (phone/address/hours/services/menu/booking link)
```

**Do not** mark a call `interested-in-demo` unless the owner clearly confirmed they want to see a preview. Mark `callback` only when a specific follow-up was agreed.

## 6. "Yes, I want to see a demo" — handoff note

If the owner says yes (any clear confirmation — "yes", "sure", "send me a demo"):

**1. Confirm the demo gate out loud (verbatim, in-voice):**
> "Glad to hear it. I'll put together a simple free preview in your own style — nothing goes live and there's no obligation. What I need from you is just a few facts so I start with the right information."

**2. Log the explicit consent** as `DEMO CONFIRMED` in the call log before handing off. Only build after this explicit confirmation — never before.

**3. Gather only verified facts, in this order (keep it short — no long questionnaire):**
- **Correct business name and spelling** — confirm exactly as it should appear
- **Preferred phone and address** — confirm the ones on their listing are current
- **Hours** — ask for the hours they want shown
- **Services** (restaurant: menu or booking link; salon: services/book a link; auto: services offered) — ask what they want customers to find first
- **Any booking / menu / social link** (Facebook, Instagram, GlossGenius) they want included
- **Photos or logo** — where to find them (their Facebook/Instagram, or ask them to send)
- **Contact name and preferred reply channel**, if they want ongoing contact (phone, text, or the email they prefer)

**4. State what you'll use and what you won't invent:**
> "I'll build the preview from the public details and the info you just gave me — if I don't have something, I won't guess at it. Take your time looking it over; nothing goes live until you approve it."

**5. Hand off** to the Builder with the Playbook's minimum handoff package:
Business name/spelling · contact name + preferred channel · exact demo confirmation · verified phone/address/hours/services/menu/booking details · public brand references + photos/logo source · any client-requested priorities · the call-log path.

**Do not** send a payment link now. Do not claim a demo is ready until there is a checked, working demo URL. Add nothing to the preview that wasn't verified or provided by the owner.
